IdiotProof — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-07-20 · Operated by MindAttic · contact ryandebraal@mindattic.com

This policy explains what data IdiotProof collects, how it is used to operate the service and connect to your brokerage account, how it is protected, and the choices you have.

1Who we are

IdiotProof is operated by MindAttic ("we," "us"). It is software that connects to your own brokerage account (Alpaca) to author and evaluate trading strategies and, when you enable it, place orders on your behalf.

2What we collect

CategoryExamples
Account & identityThe login/email you register, and authentication data needed to secure your account.
Brokerage credentialsThe Alpaca API key/secret you provide, or the OAuth token you authorize. Stored encrypted at rest and used only to operate features you enable.
Trading configurationStrategies you author, profiles, risk settings, watchlists, and preferences.
Activity & analysisEvaluations, replays, backtests, scanner results, orders/positions read from or placed through your connected account, and audit logs of gate decisions.
Market dataPrice/volume data we fetch on your behalf from the broker's data API.
Operational logsDiagnostic/error logs needed to run and secure the service. Secrets are never written to logs.

3How we use it

  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To connect to your brokerage account, evaluate your strategies against market data, and — only when you enable it — place and manage orders on your behalf.
  • To generate the replays, analytics, and dataset features you request.
  • To operate, maintain, debug, and secure the service.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your credentials for anything beyond operating the features you turn on.

4AI / automated evaluation

Some features (strategy generation and a voter-panel check on candidate trades) route through MindAttic.Legion to a large-language-model provider. Only the data needed for that specific check is sent. No order is placed on the basis of an AI response alone — every candidate must also clear your strategy's conditions and the Risk Guardian's pre-trade limits.

5How we store and protect it

Sensitive fields — brokerage credentials and OAuth tokens — are encrypted at rest using the platform's data-protection keys and are never displayed in full or logged. Runtime state (strategies, preferences, audit logs, replay records) is stored in a SQL database. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data; no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

6Who we share it with

  • Your broker (Alpaca). To read your account and place the orders you authorize, we necessarily exchange data with Alpaca. Your use of Alpaca is governed by Alpaca's own terms and privacy policy.
  • LLM provider. The minimal data needed for strategy generation / voter checks, via MindAttic.Legion.
  • Service infrastructure. Hosting/database providers that run the service under confidentiality obligations.
  • We may disclose data if required by law.

7Retention, deletion, and revocation

You can remove your stored credentials or revoke an OAuth link at any time (in IdiotProof and/or from your Alpaca dashboard). You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. We retain data only as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law.

8Your choices

You control which accounts you connect, which features you enable, and whether trading runs on paper or live. You can disconnect or delete at any time.

9Children

IdiotProof is not directed to individuals under 18 and is intended only for those legally able to open and trade a brokerage account.

10Changes & contact

We may update this policy; material changes take effect when posted here. Questions or requests: ryandebraal@mindattic.com.
